Here's what to know about Guidexa as we prepare our first launch.

Is Guidexa already fully launched?+

This website is a launch preview. Specific services, mentor availability and booking options will be confirmed as the pilot is established.

Does Guidexa guarantee a job?+

No. Guidexa aims to provide practical guidance and career-readiness support. Hiring decisions and outcomes depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.

Who is Guidexa for?+

The initial focus is students and freshers who want career clarity, skill roadmaps, mentorship or help preparing for opportunities.

Will AI make my career decisions for me?+

No. AI may help organise information and draft resources. Important recommendations should be reviewed appropriately, and you retain control over your decisions.

How much will services cost?+

Pricing is still being validated. We will publish the scope, availability and price of each service before accepting payment.
